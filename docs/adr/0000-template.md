---

## Context

What's true right now that makes this decision necessary. State what you actually verified against the codebase, not what you assumed.

## Problem

What specifically has to be decided, and what goes wrong if it isn't.

## Decision

What was decided, stated plainly.

## Alternatives considered

**The section that matters most.** For each real alternative: what it was, and why it was rejected — in enough detail that someone can tell later whether the rejection still holds. A decision with no genuine alternative wasn't worth a file; put it in `DECISIONS.md` instead.

## Tradeoffs

What's given up. Every real decision costs something.

## Risks

What could make this wrong, and what would surface it.

## Consequences

What follows — for the codebase, for future work.

## Future considerations

What to revisit, and what would trigger revisiting it.
