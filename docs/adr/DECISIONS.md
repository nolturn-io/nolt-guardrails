# Decision Log

Most decisions belong here as a short entry. A few need the full template in `0000-template.md` instead.

**Use the full template when:** there were real alternatives, and someone later will ask why they were rejected. If the decision had no genuine alternative, a log entry here is honest and a full ADR would be ceremony.

Supersede, don't rewrite. A changed decision is a new entry pointing at the old one — the record of having decided differently before is itself useful information.

---

### ADR-0001 — Example entry (delete once you have a real one)

Status: Accepted · Date: [YYYY-MM-DD]

**Context:** [one or two sentences — what was true that made this necessary]
**Decision:** [what was decided]
**Consequences:** [what follows from it]
